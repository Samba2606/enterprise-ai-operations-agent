# Demo Company Policies

## Delayed orders
If an order is delayed by more than 5 calendar days beyond its promised delivery date,
support should first verify the carrier status. If there is no confirmed delivery date,
the case may be escalated to the logistics team.

## Refunds
A full refund may be offered when an order is confirmed lost by the carrier or remains
undelivered for more than 10 calendar days after the promised delivery date.

For delays of 5 to 10 calendar days, support may offer an apology and escalate the case.
Refunds should not be issued automatically without checking the order and carrier status.

## Damaged items
Customers must report visible damage within 7 days of delivery. Support should request
an order number and a short description before starting replacement or refund review.

## Account privacy
Support agents should never reveal another customer's order, email address, phone number,
or support history.
